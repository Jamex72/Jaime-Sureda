Los Naranjos de Marbella

A setting with its own identity

Whitewashed walls, roof tiles, gardens.
A distinctly Andalusian way to live in Marbella.

Los Naranjos de Marbella sits in Nueva Andalucía, just a short distance from Puerto Banús. Its apartments and townhouses are arranged along quiet streets, landscaped gardens and communal swimming pools.

White architecture, terraces and outdoor spaces give the development a recognisable character. For an investor, that consistency must be considered alongside the phase, the condition of each property, its costs and the intended use.

Frequently asked questions

Before investing in Los Naranjos de Marbella.

What types of property can be found in Los Naranjos de Marbella?

The development includes apartments and townhouses across several phases, with Mediterranean architecture, landscaped gardens and communal swimming pools. Specific features vary by phase and property.

What should be assessed before investing?

The purchase price, property condition, community costs, documentation, applicable rules and intended use should all be reviewed before making a decision.

Can a return on investment be guaranteed?

No. Any projected return depends on the purchase price, costs, use, occupancy and applicable regulations. Each opportunity should be assessed individually and checked with independent advisers.

Does Jaime Sureda provide guidance throughout the purchase?

Yes. The service covers defining the brief, initial selection, arranging viewings and providing organised access to available information, without replacing independent legal, technical or tax advice.
