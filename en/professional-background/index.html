Professional profile

From management to real estate assets.

I am an economist and independent professional based in Marbella. My career has developed through management, leadership and service projects in Spain and Latin America, with a particular connection to golf and hospitality.

Working with teams, clients and operations in environments where service quality and profitability must move together has given me a clear way of approaching real estate: every property requires analysis, positioning and a presentation capable of expressing its true value.

Today I apply that experience to the investment and marketing of selected properties in Málaga and Marbella, with particular attention to tourist assets and opportunities that require a more complete reading than a conventional listing.
