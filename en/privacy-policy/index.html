After the relevant period, data is securely deleted or kept blocked where required by law.

Received correspondence is stored in the Proton Mail account under the applicable settings and retention policy. The professional contact register is also stored on a local hard drive controlled by the controller; no cloud CRM is currently used.

If you believe your rights have not been respected, you may complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).
