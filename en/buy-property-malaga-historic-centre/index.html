Buying guide · Málaga

Buying a home in
Málaga’s Historic Centre.

A guide to looking beyond the photographs and assessing whether a home suits your daily life or your purchase plans.

Guide prepared on 2 October 2026

One area, many different living experiences.

The Historic Centre includes shopping streets, squares and buildings with very different characteristics. A central address alone does not tell you what life inside a home is like: the specific street, orientation and relationship with businesses and courtyards matter just as much.

Before comparing listings, consider your usual journeys: work, shopping, transport and parking if you drive. Walk around the property and check the route to its entrance. A viewing should help you picture everyday life as well as understand the home.

Six things worth checking.

01

Building and maintenance

Look at the entrance, staircase, façade and shared areas. Ask about completed repairs and planned works. If you notice damp, cracks or older installations, arrange a technical review to understand their extent.

02

Lift and practical access

Check whether there is a lift, where it starts and whether steps remain between it and the home. The route from the street matters for pushchairs, luggage or mobility needs; a listing’s lift checkbox is not enough.

03

Light and ventilation

Identify which rooms face the street or a courtyard. Visit in daylight, open windows and assess ventilation. A higher floor does not automatically mean more light: orientation and neighbouring buildings also matter.

04

Noise and rest

Visit at different times, including the evening if sleep is a priority. Listen from the bedrooms and examine windows and glazing. The city council publishes information on acoustically saturated areas; check the specific surroundings rather than assuming the entire centre has the same noise levels.

05

Layout and condition

Check whether your furniture fits and how rooms connect. Review the kitchen, bathrooms, heating and cooling, and finishes. If you plan refurbishment, assess feasibility and budget before factoring it into your decision.

06

Community and costs

Ask about regular community fees, included services and approved or proposed additional contributions. Request community information and budget for maintenance and the acquisition costs applicable to your purchase.

Your purpose changes what matters.

As your home

Prioritise rest, accessibility, storage and daily journeys. A location appealing for a short stay may not meet the needs of a main residence.

As a second home

Consider maintenance while you are away, access with luggage and arrangements for checking the property. Ask who would deal with an issue during your absence.

As an investment

Define the intended use and review documents, costs and income assumptions. If your project depends on a particular rental or use, confirm its feasibility before committing. Location does not guarantee a return.

Documents to support an informed decision.

Request an up-to-date land registry extract (nota simple) and review ownership, the registered description and charges with your adviser. Compare advertised floor areas and layouts with the available documents and clarify any differences.

Request information on the community, occupancy and previous alterations. If the building is protected or you plan changes, check the conditions applying to the specific property before budgeting for works. This guide helps structure a viewing; property-specific questions are resolved through documentation and the relevant professionals.

Questions to take to a viewing.

  • Which rooms face the street and which face a courtyard?
  • How does noise change during the day and at night?
  • Are there steps between the street, lift and home?
  • What alterations were made and what information is available about them?
  • What are the community fees, and are works or additional contributions planned?
  • Which floor area is advertised, and which document supports it?
  • Will the home be handed over vacant, and on what terms?
  • What remains to be checked for my intended use?
