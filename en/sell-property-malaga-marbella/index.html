Frequently asked questions

Before putting it on the market.

What information is needed to value a property?

Location, floor area, layout, condition, current use and available documentation allow an initial valuation to be prepared. Specific information is reviewed confidentially.

Can a property be sold while tourist activity is ongoing?

Each case requires a review of bookings, contracts, management and documentation. The strategy is defined after analysing these circumstances and with the appropriate legal advice.

Does Jaime Sureda work with properties in Málaga and Marbella?

Yes. The acquisition, presentation and marketing service focuses on selected properties in Málaga and Marbella.

Does a valuation oblige me to list the property for sale?

No. The initial conversation and analysis are used to understand the property and assess whether there is a suitable fit before agreeing any marketing strategy.
