Preparing to sell

What to review before listing your home.

Price and objectives. Explain when you would like to sell and any circumstances affecting the transaction. A price proposal should consider the specific location, condition and competing properties, alongside floor area.

Consistent information. Gather the documents and details you already have. Differences in floor area, alterations or an occupied home should be clarified before preparing the property page.

Presentation and viewings. Tidying, decluttering and addressing small details can help present the home. Photographs should reflect its actual condition; viewing availability is agreed with the owner.

Frequently asked questions

Before putting it on the market.

How is the asking price for my home assessed?

Location, floor area, condition, layout and comparable competing properties are reviewed. An advertised price alone does not establish the value of a completed sale. The proposal is discussed with the owner before marketing starts.

What information do I need to get started?

For the first conversation, share the location, type of home, its condition, whether it is occupied and your sales objective. Available documents and floor areas are then reviewed to prepare a consistent presentation.

How long does it take to sell an apartment in Málaga or Marbella?

Timing depends on price, location, condition, demand and the terms of the transaction. There is no guaranteed sales period. Enquiries, viewings and offers help inform reviews of the marketing strategy.

Do I need to renovate before selling?

This depends on the home’s condition and intended buyer. The current presentation is reviewed first, distinguishing small adjustments from larger works. Any expenditure is agreed with the owner.

How are fees and terms agreed?

Before work begins, the services, fees, marketing channels and terms of the instruction are agreed. This allows the owner to assess the proposal before making commitments.

Can the sale of a rented home or a property used for holiday rentals be considered?

The initial review considers occupancy, contracts, bookings and available documentation. The specific circumstances are assessed before agreeing viewings and a sales strategy, with legal advice where appropriate.

Does the first enquiry commit me to selling my home?

No. The initial conversation is used to understand the property and your objectives. Marketing terms are agreed afterwards if both parties decide to proceed.
