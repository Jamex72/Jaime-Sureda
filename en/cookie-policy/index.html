No cookies in use

1. Current website status

jaimesureda.com does not currently set first- or third-party cookies and does not use browser local storage.

There are no analytics tools, advertising pixels, user profiles, embedded chat, embedded videos or embedded maps. A consent banner is therefore not displayed.

The language switch works only while the page is open and is not saved after closing it or moving to another page.

2. What cookies are

Cookies are small files or pieces of information stored on a device by a website or third party to remember data, provide functions, measure use or personalise content. Other technologies, such as local storage, may serve similar purposes.

Strictly necessary technical cookies may be exempt from consent. Analytics, advertising or unsolicited personalisation cookies require clear information and, as a rule, prior consent.

3. Technologies used

CookiesNone.
Local storageNot used.
Analytics or advertisingNot used.
Fonts and resourcesVisual resources and fonts are served from the website itself.
Technical logsInfrastructure providers may generate connection and security logs. These are not cookies, and their processing is explained in the Privacy policy.

4. Links to third-party services

The website contains links to WhatsApp, email and maps. These services are not loaded within the page. They are only contacted when a person selects the link and leaves jaimesureda.com; their own policies and settings apply from that moment.

5. Future changes

If tools using non-essential cookies or technologies are added in the future—for example analytics, advertising or embedded external content—this policy will be updated and those tools will be blocked until a valid choice is obtained.

The consent panel will present accept and reject with equal visibility, allow category settings and make withdrawal easy. This policy will be reviewed when the technology or applicable law changes.
