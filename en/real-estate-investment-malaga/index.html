Assessing an opportunity

The purchase price is only part of the analysis.

Total cost. Alongside the price, identify the acquisition costs applicable to the transaction, any refurbishment and the furnishings required. Community fees, maintenance, insurance and management also belong in the budget.

Income and expenses. If you plan to rent, separate projected income from operating expenses. Compare scenarios with different occupancy levels, periods without income and repairs; estimates should state their assumptions and supporting data.

Intended use. The physical layout and your intended use should be considered alongside the property’s documentation. If your project depends on a specific use, clarify that question before deciding.

Frequently asked questions

Before investing.

How do I start investing in a home in Málaga?

Define your total budget, intended use and investment horizon. Then compare properties by location, condition, layout and documentation. A viewing and a review of costs help make the assessment more concrete.

What costs should I consider beyond the price?

Estimate the acquisition costs applicable to your transaction, refurbishment and furnishings. Include relevant community fees, maintenance, insurance and management costs. Specific amounts are reviewed for each transaction.

How are potential investment returns assessed?

Compare estimated income with expenses and the total investment. Identify the sources of the data and the assumptions used, including periods without income and repairs. A projection does not guarantee results.

How do Panaderos and Fernando Camino differ?

Panaderos 10 is an apartment in Málaga’s Historic Centre. Fernando Camino 15 is in La Malagueta and has two functional units within a single registered property. Their pages detail floor areas, layouts and asking prices.

Can I consider holiday rental use for the property?

Available information about each property is presented on its page and checked against documentation during the purchase assessment. The intended use requires a specific review; a commercial reference to tourist registration does not replace that assessment.
