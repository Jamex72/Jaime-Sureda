Buying guide · Málaga

Buying a home in
La Malagueta, Málaga.

What to check when choosing a home near the beach for everyday living, a second home or an investment project.

Guide prepared on 2 October 2026

Living near the sea means looking at the details too.

La Malagueta combines an urban beach with proximity to Málaga’s Historic Centre. The actual walk from the entrance to the beach and the services you use matters more than an approximate distance in a listing. Walk around the area and check access routes and crossings.

Not every home has sea views or the same level of quiet. Orientation, floor level, neighbouring buildings and the specific street can change the experience considerably. Compare the rooms where you will spend most time as well as the building’s location.

Six checks to help you choose.

01

Orientation, light and views

Visit in daylight and observe sunlight in the living room and bedrooms. Distinguish direct sea views, side views and proximity to the beach without views. Check the buildings opposite and how the rooms ventilate.

02

Noise and windows

Listen from the rooms with windows open and closed. Compare different times if the home faces traffic, businesses or busy routes. Review glazing and heating and cooling before assessing comfort.

03

Lift and accessibility

Follow the full route from the street: entrance steps, lift size and access to the home. Ask about maintenance and planned works; a building with a lift does not necessarily provide step-free access.

04

Parking and garage

If you need a car, check the actual options. Where a space is offered, confirm whether it is included, its location and the terms of the right being transferred. Visit with your vehicle to check access and manoeuvring; the word “garage” does not establish outright ownership.

05

Building and exposure to the sea

Look at the façade, balconies, railings, windows and shared areas. Ask about maintenance and repairs. If you notice deterioration, stains or corrosion, arrange a technical review to understand their cause and extent; do not automatically attribute them to the sea.

06

Community and budget

Request regular community fees and information on additional contributions and planned works. Compare included services and budget for maintenance, potential improvements and acquisition costs. A low fee alone does not explain the building’s condition.

Priorities depend on your intended use.

Main residence

Check rest, storage, accessibility and routes to shops and transport. Visit the area at different times to assess the street’s rhythm.

Second home

Consider access with luggage, maintenance during absences and who would handle an issue. Plan how to ventilate and check the home while you are away.

Investment

Define the intended use and review documents, costs and income assumptions. Proximity to the beach does not establish the feasibility of a particular rental use or guarantee returns.

Documents before committing.

Review an up-to-date land registry extract (nota simple) with your adviser and compare ownership, charges, floor area and description with the commercial presentation. If the layout includes several units, clarify the registered status and exactly what is being sold.

Request information on the community, occupancy and previous alterations. For any use or works essential to your decision, confirm the conditions applying to that property. If a garage is offered separately, review its documentation and price separately.

Questions for your viewing.

  • What views are there from each room, beyond the framing of photographs?
  • How does the light change during the day, and which way does the home face?
  • Can traffic or activity from businesses be heard in the bedrooms?
  • Are there steps between the street and the front door?
  • What façade, balcony or lift works have been completed or are planned?
  • Are additional contributions approved, and what services do community fees cover?
  • Is parking included, and what right is being transferred?
  • Do the layout and floor area correspond to the available documents?
